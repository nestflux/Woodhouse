We're looking for a Senior Frontend Engineer to join our Dashboard team. You'll build the interfaces that millions of businesses use to manage their payments, subscriptions, and financial operations. This is a high-impact role where your work directly affects how businesses interact with Stripe.

  • Excellent communication skills and ability to work cross-functionally

Nice to Have

  • Experience with GraphQL and data visualization libraries
  • Contributions to open-source projects
  • Experience in fintech or payments
